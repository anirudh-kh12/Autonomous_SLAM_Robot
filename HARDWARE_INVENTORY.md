## Inventory Notes

- Confirm the exact model before recording a specification.
- Check voltage, current, interface and compatibility.
- Record whether a component is available permanently or only temporarily.
- Do not connect unknown components to a power supply until their ratings are established.
- Add additional rows as components are discovered.
